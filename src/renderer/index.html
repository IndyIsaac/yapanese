Setup

Yapanese needs two more things

Transcription is done by whisper.cpp — a separate program — and a speech model, the file that actually knows what words sound like. Neither ships with the installer: together they are far larger than the app, and which build is right depends on this machine.

Fetched once, then it all runs offline.

Engine

whisper.cpp is the program that turns recorded audio into text. It runs on this machine and does the actual listening — Yapanese records your voice, hands it over, and types back what it heard.

Both builds below produce the same words. The difference is what does the work: your processor, or your graphics card. The NVIDIA build is bigger because it carries NVIDIA's own maths libraries with it.

Optional

Prefer to do this yourself? Put whisper-cli.exe on your PATH or in the yap bin folder, and the models in the models folder. Yapanese will find them. Every download here is pinned to a published release and checked against a SHA-256 before it is installed.
